# Contributing

Thanks for helping make job-search support available to more people.

- Report outdated sources, broken links, confusing instructions, or suspected invented claims.
- Suggest an original workflow only if you have the rights to share it. Read [SOURCE_POLICY.md](SOURCE_POLICY.md).
- Include publication date, geography, population, exact denominator, and original link when reporting statistics.
- Don't upload resumes, names/emails of job seekers, private recruiter messages, paid-course content, or identifiable personal account promo screenshots.
- No mass messaging, unauthorized scraping, impersonation, or promises of interview outcomes.
- Treat AI and automated source discoveries as drafts. A human must verify promotional terms and source methods.
- Prefer a shorter, clearer instruction that a first-time user can follow over elaborate jargon.

We welcome corrections. Sources and limits matter more than clever-sounding advice.
