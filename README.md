# Job Search Survival Kit

**A practical, free, copy-and-paste toolkit for people looking for work. No paywall. No fake ATS scores. No job-offer guarantees.**

You do **not** need to know Git, write code, or pay for LinkedIn Premium. You can use these files in your browser.

## Start in 5 minutes: one job, one action plan

**[OPEN THE ONE-JOB QUICKSTART →](START_HERE.md)**. It uses a single copy-and-paste prompt to decode the role, assess your evidence, suggest honest resume edits, plan outreach, and prepare for interview questions. [See a fictional worked example](examples/FICTIONAL-EXAMPLE.md).

## Three-minute instructions

1. Open the relevant guide above.
2. Copy the prompt under **Copy this prompt**.
3. Paste it into the AI assistant you already use. Fill in your resume, job description, or other requested details. **Do not upload confidential information you do not want that service to have.**
4. Check the results against the actual job posting and your real experience. Ask the assistant to correct anything it guessed.
5. Take the next real-world action: apply, reach out, practice, or decide to skip the role.

**AI optional:** You can use the checklists manually. Some tools offer free usage with limits, but this repository does not require a paid subscription.

## What this toolkit does NOT do

- Guarantee a job, interview, referral, or any particular outcome.
- Claim to know the employer's private ATS ranking, hiring-manager scorecard, or screening algorithm.
- Create fake metrics, experience, credentials, contacts, or salary data.
- Encourage mass unsolicited messages or automated job applications.
- Ask you to upload private resumes or store applicant data in this public repo.

## Research that updates, without inventing data

The [Market Evidence Watch](guides/market-evidence-watch.md) is a GitHub Actions workflow that checks specified official sources weekly for newly linked reports. It drafts a **pull request for human review**, not automatic changes to published hiring statistics. This protects against bad extraction, marketing spin, and contradictory numbers.

Current sources: Greenhouse, Ashby, Indeed Hiring Lab, LinkedIn Workforce Reports, and the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Each published statistic must include the source, date, sample, geography, and meaning of its denominator. **A board's observed hire rate is not your personal chance of getting hired.**

## AI subscription promotion watch

The [AI Subscription Deal Watch](promos/ai-subscription-savings.md) includes a no-paywall savings checklist and a separate [daily GitHub Action](.github/workflows/ai-subscription-promo-watch.yml). It monitors **public official pages** from OpenAI, Perplexity, Anthropic/Claude, and PayPal. Newly linked pages and changed promotional language are queued for **human review** in a pull request. Nothing is automatically announced as verified, no offer is automatically redeemed, and account-only discounts cannot be observed. The first run records a baseline.

**Important:** a deal advertised as 50% cashback may still bill the full price upfront. A PayPal Debit card is not automatically the same thing as redeeming an offer through the PayPal app. Confirm activation and the payment terms before changing subscriptions.
