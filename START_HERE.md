# Start here: one job posting, one practical action plan

**Time needed:** 5–10 minutes. **Cost:** $0 for this toolkit. **Coding:** none. You can use a free AI assistant, subject to that provider's usage limits.

This is the shortest path through Career OS. **Don't try every workflow. Start with one real job.**

## Before you begin

You need:

1. A job description copied from the employer's website or another legitimate listing.
2. Your current resume **or** a rough list of jobs, responsibilities, and results. Bullet points are fine.
3. Any non-negotiables, such as pay, location, schedule, or travel. Sharing these is optional.

**Already built your master career record?** Paste the relevant parts of that private record into the experience field below instead of reconstructing your work history. If you haven't, don't delay today's application: this quickstart works with rough notes too. When you have time, [build your master resume once](guides/master-resume-source-of-truth.md).

**Privacy check:** Remove your home address, phone number, personal email, ID numbers, references' contact information, confidential client data, and anything else you would not want to share with your chosen AI service. Do not paste protected health information. Your inputs are not collected by this GitHub repository, but an external AI assistant has its own data policies.

## 1. Copy this entire prompt

```text
I am applying for work and need an honest, practical assessment, not encouragement or a fake ATS score. Use only the job posting and career information I supply. Do not invent facts or claim access to any employer's confidential hiring process.

JOB POSTING:
[PASTE THE FULL JOB DESCRIPTION]

MY RESUME OR CAREER HISTORY:
[PASTE YOUR RESUME OR ROUGH EXPERIENCE NOTES]

MY NON-NEGOTIABLES (OPTIONAL):
[PAY, LOCATION, SCHEDULE, TRAVEL, OTHER REAL CONSTRAINTS]

Produce an easy-to-scan ONE-JOB ACTION PLAN with these sections:

1. THE VERDICT (3 sentences max): Apply, Investigate, or Skip, and why. A missing preferred qualification does not automatically mean Skip.
2. WHAT THEY NEED: The five most important requirements the posting actually states. Quote or closely reference the posting and label inferred priorities separately.
3. MY EVIDENCE: The three strongest relevant examples from my supplied experience. Cite my exact source wording. If evidence is missing, say "not established."
4. MY GAPS: Separate actual missing qualifications from information that I simply did not provide. Suggest honest ways to address transferable experience, without pretending I meet a requirement I don't.
5. RESUME CHANGES: Rewrite my professional summary and at most three accomplishment bullets for this role. Keep my true titles, dates, and responsibilities. Never invent metrics. Label any unsupported detail [VERIFY BEFORE USING].
6. REACH A HUMAN: Recommend the most relevant kind of contact (function/title, not an invented person) and write one direct, friendly outreach message of 60–90 words. If no contact is identifiable, give specific steps to find a legitimate contact. Do not recommend scraping or spam.
7. INTERVIEW PREP: Three likely questions, labeled as hypotheses, and the evidence I should prepare for each.
8. NEXT THREE ACTIONS: A realistic order of operations I can complete today. Include a "stop/skip" recommendation when this role is not worth my time.

RULES:
- Never fabricate achievements, salary benchmarks, contact information, employer policies, or a percentage likelihood of getting hired.
- Do not assume AI automatically rejected my application.
- Separate FACTS from INFERENCES. Flag a missing job-description detail rather than guessing.
- Do not use unrelated sensitive personal characteristics in the evaluation.
- Keep the entire response below 900 words and write in plain English.
- If I supplied no real job history, give me a short fill-in template instead of pretending to assess fit.
```

## 2. Paste it into the AI assistant you already use

Most assistants have a free tier, but limits and privacy terms differ. You do **not** need ChatGPT Plus, LinkedIn Premium, or another paid subscription to use this repository.

## 3. Verify before you use anything

Check every rewritten sentence against your own experience and the actual job posting. In particular, verify job titles, years, numbers, credentials, and whether an employer actually requires a qualification. **An AI-generated match percentage is not a reliable ATS score.**

## 4. Do one real-world thing

Apply, request a legitimate introduction, send the outreach message after personalizing it, practice your interview answer aloud, or skip a poor-fit job. The output is only useful when it informs an action.

### Don't have a resume yet?

Replace the resume field with:

```text
Most recent job: [title, employer type, approximate dates]
What I did: [3–5 tasks]
What I improved or helped deliver: [specific examples; numbers only if known]
Previous experience: [roles and transferable skills]
Training and credentials: [only what is current and accurate]
```
