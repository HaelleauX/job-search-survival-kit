# Hiring-market evidence: read the denominator before the headline

**Last editorial verification:** November 23, 2026

## Greenhouse: *The Hire Standard*, Fall 2026 (U.S.)

**Primary source:** [Greenhouse recruiting benchmarks](https://www.greenhouse.com/recruiting-benchmarks); *The Hire Standard, Fall 2026*, pp. 2, 15–16, 42–45. This repository summarizes facts and links to the publisher; it does not reproduce the report.

**Sample:** 9,473 U.S. organizations using Greenhouse; 161.8 million applications and 831,933 hires; January–August 2026. Rates are **observed across these Greenhouse customers**, not all job seekers or employers.

| Metric | Reported figure | Meaning / caveat |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Application-to-hire rate (overall) | 0.54% | Across observed Greenhouse applications, not an individual's personal chance |
| Applications reaching first interview | 3.2% | Approximately 32 of 1,000 applications |
| Average days to fill role | 61.6 | From requisition open to accepted offer, **not** from candidate application |
| Average days between applying and first interview | 19 | Average for applicants who reached the first interview |
| Applications per remote posting | 415 | Compared with 128 on-site and 206 hybrid |
| Applications per remote hire | 301.1 | Compared with 128.9 on-site and 259.3 hybrid |

### Job-board source conversion (Appendix A10, p. 44)

| Source label in report | Applications per hire | Observed hire conversion |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Indeed – sponsored | 82 | 1.216% |
| LinkedIn Limited Listing | 105 | 0.951% |
| Indeed | 198 | 0.506% |
| ZipRecruiter | 297 | 0.336% |
| LinkedIn | 673 | 0.149% |
| LinkedIn (ad posting) | 785 | 0.127% |

**Interpretation:** In the Greenhouse sample, standard LinkedIn listings generated relatively low conversion to hires compared with standard Indeed listings. This does **not** show that LinkedIn Premium causes or prevents hiring, and the groups may differ in role types and candidate selection. Also, "LinkedIn Limited Listing" is a **specific employer listing type**, not a subscription available to job seekers; its strong average was not consistent across all company sizes.

### Sourcing channel comparison (Appendix A9, p. 43)

- Referrals: **17.2 applications per hire**, 0.8% of applications and 10.7% of hires.
- Recruiter outreach: **39.7 applications per hire**, 1.6% of applications and 8.7% of hires.
- External job boards: **306.9 applications per hire**, 43.1% of applications and 31.3% of hires.
- Company career sites ("internal job board" in the report): **312.4 applications per hire**, 51.9% of applications and 37.0% of hires.

**Be precise:** these observational sourcing rates do not prove that a referral makes any *particular* candidate 18 times likelier to receive an offer. They support trying to gain legitimate access to human decision-makers while still applying to relevant jobs.

### What we do NOT infer

- No ATS software's private algorithm or thresholds are disclosed here.
- The 92.5% of applications not reaching an assessment is **not evidence** that an AI system automatically rejected 92.5%.
- A remote role's higher application volume does not mean applicants in every occupation face the same competition.
- An open role that closes without a hire is not necessarily fake.

## How new data is published here

A bot can identify **new report links**, but a human must verify sample, date, geography, exact metric definition and limitations. Different publishers are often measuring different applicant populations. We do not merge unlike numbers into a misleading "average chance of hiring."
