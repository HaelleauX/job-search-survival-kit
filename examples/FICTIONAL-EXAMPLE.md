# Example: use Career OS with a fictional job seeker

**This is a fictional demonstration**, not an actual employer posting, user testimonial,
or observed hiring result. Every name, company, and achievement below is invented for
teaching purposes. Do not copy these achievements into your own resume.

## Fictional posting

**Operations Coordinator | Example Services Co. | Hybrid, 2 days/week**

We need someone to coordinate client onboarding, schedule project milestones, keep
accurate CRM records, resolve handoff issues with sales and support, and report weekly
progress. Required: two years in customer operations or project coordination,
spreadsheet proficiency, and strong written communication. Preferred: Salesforce
experience and healthcare industry familiarity.

## Fictional applicant

**Jordan (fictional)** worked as a library program coordinator for three years.
Coordinated 25 community events, kept registration records in spreadsheets, and
worked with volunteers and partner organizations. Jordan has never used Salesforce
and has no healthcare experience. Jordan needs a mostly remote position.

## What a useful result should look like

**Verdict: Investigate.** Jordan appears to meet coordination and spreadsheet
requirements through transferable experience, but the hybrid schedule might conflict
with Jordan's work preference. Confirm the office days and commute before investing
much more time.

**Requirement evidence:**

| Posting requirement | Jordan's evidence | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2 years of coordination | Three years coordinating library programs | Transferable, substantively relevant |
| Spreadsheet proficiency | Maintained registration records in spreadsheets | Evidenced, level needs clarification |
| Written communication | No specific writing example supplied | Not established |
| Salesforce | Has not used it | Preferred-skill gap |
| Healthcare familiarity | No healthcare experience | Preferred-skill gap |

**Resume bullet, if accurate:** “Coordinated 25 community events, maintaining
registration records and collaborating with volunteers and partner organizations.”

**Not acceptable:** “Led 25 enterprise CRM implementations and increased client
retention by 40%.” Nothing supplied supports that claim.

**Potential outreach:** Reach out to a real recruiter or operations manager listed
on the company's official site or a verifiable professional profile. Don't invent an
employee's identity or pretend Jordan has a referral.

**Next actions:** (1) Check commute requirements; (2) prepare a genuine example of
written stakeholder communication; (3) apply if the schedule works.
