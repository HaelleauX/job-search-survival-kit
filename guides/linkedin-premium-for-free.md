# LinkedIn Premium for $0: legitimate trials, promos and referral offers

**Last verified: November 23, 2026.** Availability and eligibility vary by account, location, plan and promotion. A free trial is not a guarantee and does not confer a better chance of getting hired.

## Where to look

1. **Your own LinkedIn account:** look for a Premium trial or promotional banner. LinkedIn's help page documents **Me → Settings & Privacy → Account preferences → Subscriptions & payments → Upgrade for Free** for eligible accounts. The exact UI can change.
2. **Offers from people you know:** LinkedIn says members can receive promotional links from connections. Verify the offer on LinkedIn itself before entering payment information. Never give another person your password or card number to "activate" an offer.
3. **Referral promotions:** LinkedIn documents a **two-month free Sales Navigator referral offer** for eligible recipients. This is *not the same as two free months of Premium Business*, and the published terms do **not** promise that the sender gets an extra two months. Availability of referrals is conditional.
4. **Official partners:** sometimes promotions are available through partner programs. Verify each on an official LinkedIn or partner page and read the eligibility rules. Don't buy questionable "free trial" codes.

## The free-trial checklist

- [ ] Confirm the **exact product** (Premium Career, Premium Business, Sales Navigator, etc.).
- [ ] Confirm **who** gets the free months (recipient, referrer or both).
- [ ] Screenshot the offer, eligible dates, renewal price, and full terms *before* activation.
- [ ] Look up the actual renewal/charge date under your subscription settings.
- [ ] Add a calendar reminder **3 days before renewal**, plus a backup reminder **2 days before renewal**. LinkedIn says cancel at least **one day before** the billing date to avoid charges; an earlier reminder adds breathing room.
- [ ] Decide whether to cancel at that reminder; verify the cancellation confirmation and remaining access.

**Important:** LinkedIn states that canceling a free trial early may immediately end Premium benefits and can affect eligibility for another trial for at least 12 months. Don't automatically assume you can cancel on day one and keep benefits until expiration. The correct timing depends on the promotion and its terms.

## Calendar event you can create

**Event:** Decide/cancel LinkedIn Premium before renewal

**When:** [actual billing date] minus 3 calendar days (set an additional reminder 2 days before)

**Notes:** Check subscription plan and auto-renewal date. Confirm cancellation or consciously approve the paid price. Keep the offer screenshot and cancellation confirmation. If purchased through Apple/Google, check the subscription there.
