# LinkedIn without paying for Premium

**Use LinkedIn as a public professional directory and a networking channel, not as a tollbooth.** Free LinkedIn can still support profile editing, following companies, applying to jobs, and connection requests. Some messaging and search features have limits.

## Free-use plan

1. Make the first two lines of your About section concrete: who you help, what problems you solve, and proof.
2. Research roles on LinkedIn, then verify every opening on the employer's own career site.
3. Look for people in the relevant team. Start with real shared connections and professional context.
4. Send a short, credible connection note when available; avoid bulk or generic pitches.
5. If a role is worth applying to, use the job posting's requirements to tailor the top half of your resume accurately.
6. Track meaningful replies, not profile views or notification counts.

## Why we don't equate visibility with hiring

Greenhouse's Fall 2026 U.S. report lists **0.149% hire conversion from standard LinkedIn applications** and **0.127% from LinkedIn ad postings** in its Greenhouse customer data. Standard Indeed applications were **0.506%**. These are *source-level, observational rates* from a platform sample, not your personal probability of hiring, and they do not mean Premium will improve your odds. See [the methods and original report](../evidence/hiring-market-numbers.md).

**No evidence-backed requirement says you must buy Premium to be considered.** If you're paying, decide which specific feature is valuable *to you*, not whether a badge looks impressive.
