# Check your fit without a fake score

**You need:** a job description and your real experience, ideally drawn from a [private master career record](../guides/master-resume-source-of-truth.md) (an ordinary resume or rough notes also work). **You get:** evidence-backed strengths, missing requirements, and a practical Apply / Investigate / Skip decision. A made-up 92% ATS score is not useful.

## Copy this prompt

```text
Evaluate whether I should apply for this role. You have no access to the employer's private selection criteria. Use only the documents I provide.

JOB POSTING:
[PASTE]

MY BASELINE RESUME / RELEVANT MASTER CAREER RECORD:
[PASTE REAL EXPERIENCE; REMOVE PRIVATE CONTACT DETAILS AND CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION]

Classify each top requirement as:
- EVIDENCED: clear proof from my background (quote the proof)
- POSSIBLY TRANSFERABLE: comparable experience; explain the bridge
- NOT ESTABLISHED: missing or ambiguous evidence
- GAP: I truly do not meet it

Then give:
1. Best evidence for my candidacy (maximum 5 points).
2. Actual gaps and plausible mitigations, without pretending gaps don't exist.
3. Three questions I should answer to improve accuracy.
4. Apply / investigate / skip recommendation with specific reasons.
5. The top two aspects I should emphasize if I apply.

Never fabricate achievements, credentials, hiring rules, or ATS scores. Do not use personal background irrelevant to the job.
```
