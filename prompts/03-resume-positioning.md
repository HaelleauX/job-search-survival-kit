# Improve your resume, not your fiction

**You need:** an existing resume and target job description. **You get:** a credible revised summary and bullets you can copy into your preferred document editor.

## Copy this prompt

```text
You are a rigorous resume editor. Improve how clearly my experience matches this role without adding anything untrue.

JOB POSTING:
[PASTE]

RESUME:
[PASTE]

ADDITIONAL VERIFIED ACHIEVEMENTS (OPTIONAL):
[PASTE]

RULES:
- Never invent jobs, titles, dates, software, degrees, certifications, metrics or results.
- Mark unsupported numbers as [VERIFY] rather than guessing.
- Keep company/job chronology and official titles accurate.
- Translate transferable experience into familiar role language only when evidence supports it.
- Prefer concrete outcomes and scope over generic leadership adjectives.
- Keep formatting simple and parseable; avoid columns, icons and decorative tables in the resume.

RETURN:
1. Top three issues blocking an immediate understanding of my fit.
2. A revised 2–3 sentence professional summary.
3. Up to six rewritten accomplishment bullets, each with the original evidence it came from.
4. Missing evidence/questions I should resolve before using the output.
5. A short recruiter skim test: what will they understand within 10 seconds?

Don't rewrite parts of the resume that aren't relevant. Never claim a guaranteed ATS pass.
```
