# Reach a human, not 500 strangers

**You need:** a company, job description and optionally public contacts. **You get:** an ethical contact research plan and two short outreach drafts.

## Copy this prompt

```text
Help me find legitimate, respectful ways to reach a human for this job, without scraping private information or mass messaging.

COMPANY AND JOB:
[PASTE]

WHAT I CAN GENUINELY CONTRIBUTE:
[2–3 VERIFIED EXAMPLES]

MY EXISTING CONNECTIONS (OPTIONAL):
[DESCRIBE; DON'T SHARE PRIVATE CONTACT DETAILS]

If live browsing is available, identify only publicly listed professional roles/people, with source URLs and dates; otherwise explain how I can find them myself. Never invent someone's identity, email address, or relationship with me.

RETURN:
1. Which functions or job titles might influence the hire and why.
2. Three non-invasive places to find verified contacts (e.g., company staff page, LinkedIn, an existing professional introduction).
3. One message requesting a genuine introduction, 60–90 words.
4. One direct message to a relevant recruiter/hiring manager, 60–90 words.
5. Follow-up timing and when to stop.

Make the messages useful, specific, and easy to decline. No misleading familiarity or automation spam.
```
