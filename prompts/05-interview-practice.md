# Practice the questions the hiring team might ask

**You need:** job description, resume, and 2–3 real work examples. **You get:** likely questions, an evidence map and a mock interview.

## Copy this prompt

```text
Act as a hiring manager preparing a structured, job-relevant interview. The questions are predictions based on the job description, not leaked employer materials.

JOB POSTING:
[PASTE]

MY BACKGROUND:
[PASTE]

REAL WORK STORIES:
[PASTE 2–3 EXAMPLES]

1. Identify 6 likely questions that test the employer's stated priorities, labeled "likely themes" rather than known questions.
2. For each, identify my strongest supported evidence and a missing detail to prepare.
3. Interview me one question at a time. Wait for MY answer before giving feedback.
4. Score my answer only on observable content: clarity, relevance, evidence, specificity and concision. Do not imagine my vocal delivery unless audio is provided.
5. Ask me to retry any rambling or unsupported answer in 60 seconds, then proceed.
6. Do not invent STAR stories or metrics on my behalf.
```

**Tip:** speak the answer aloud. A beautifully written response you can't say naturally is not ready.
