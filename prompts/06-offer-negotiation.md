# Evaluate an offer without getting bullied by a spreadsheet

**You need:** the written offer (without identifiers), your priorities, and public compensation sources if available.

## Copy this prompt

```text
Help me evaluate a job offer fairly and prepare a concise negotiation. Do not invent market salary data.

ROLE, LOCATION AND WORK MODEL:
[PASTE]

OFFER DETAILS (REDACT PRIVATE INFORMATION):
[BASE, BONUS, EQUITY, BENEFITS, PTO, TRAVEL, SCHEDULE]

WHAT MATTERS MOST TO ME:
[PRIORITIES]

MY SUPPORTED RESULTS:
[2–3 EXAMPLES]

1. Summarize what is known and missing.
2. Separate guaranteed pay from variable/conditional compensation.
3. Flag important offer terms to clarify before signing.
4. If you can browse current compensation sources, link them and explain sample/role differences. Otherwise do not invent a market range.
5. Draft a courteous, concrete negotiation reply with one clear ask and a collaborative tone.
6. Include a graceful alternative if the employer cannot move on base salary.
```
