# A simple job-search tracker

You can keep this in a note, document, paper planner, or spreadsheet. No software required.

| Applied | Company / role | Work model | Source | Referral/contact | Status | Next follow-up | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| YYYY-MM-DD | Example Co / Implementation Specialist | Remote | Career site | None yet | Applied | YYYY-MM-DD | Pending |

**Review once a week:**

- Which source is producing meaningful recruiter conversations?
- Are the roles in which you are getting interest different from those with instant rejections?
- What percentage of applications have had enough time to receive a response? Treat the first 2–3 weeks as a pending cohort, not a final rejection rate.
- What is one next action that improves access to a real person?

Do not paste personal contact lists, interviews or resumes into a public GitHub issue.
